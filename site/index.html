Claude Code · updated in 1.2

Know what Claude is doing, without checking

Hand Claude Code a long task and get on with your day. Corner shows every session in the corner of your screen by its chat's name, and taps you on the shoulder the moment Claude needs an answer or is done. Click a session, or its notification, to jump straight to that chat in the Claude app.

  • WorkingViolet, with how long it's been running. Your own timers and meetings still come first.
  • Needs youAmber as soon as Claude asks for permission or a reply, with one chime and a notification.
  • DoneGreen when a turn finishes, with the start of Claude's reply. Quick answers stay quiet.

One click in Settings adds Corner's hooks to Claude Code, with a backup of your settings. Events stay in a file on your Mac, and the hook takes milliseconds, so it never holds Claude up. Works with the Claude Code CLI and the Claude app's Code tab.

Seven themes tint the glass and give every section its own color, in light and dark mode. Red for alerts and amber for "soon" never change, so a warning always looks like one.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Will it cover the buttons in the corner of my windows?

It can, so choose Shrink, grow on hover or Tuck into the edge under Settings → "When not in use". Tucked, it's a 10-point dot and clicks go straight through. Push the pointer into the left edge of the screen to bring it back.

Does it work in full-screen apps and on every desktop?

Yes. It shows on every Space, and moves to the very top when an app is full screen and the menu bar is hidden.

Why does macOS say it can't check Corner?

Corner isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS says “Apple could not verify ‘Corner’ is free of malware”. Click Done, then allow it once in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (step by step). You won't be asked again for that version. Or install with curl -fsSL https://corner-mac.vercel.app/install.sh | sh in Terminal, which macOS doesn't ask about.

Why does it ask for calendar access again after an update?

Without an Apple developer certificate, macOS treats each new version as a new app for permissions. Click Connect in the card again.

⌥⌘T does something else in one of my apps

While Corner runs, ⌥⌘T opens quick add everywhere. In Finder that's normally Hide Toolbar. Quick add is also in the menu bar menu and the corner's right-click menu.

What does the Claude Code setup change?

It adds six hooks to ~/.claude/settings.json (saving a backup first) that each run a tiny script. The script writes the event to a file in Corner's own folder and exits, so nothing leaves your Mac and Claude never waits on it. Settings → Claude Code → Remove takes Corner's entries back out and leaves the rest alone. To name sessions and open their chats, Corner also reads the Claude app's list of chats on your Mac; it never changes it.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit from the menu bar icon, delete Corner from Applications, and delete ~/Library/Application Support/Corner to remove your saved timers and to-dos.
