If macOS says “Apple could not verify Corner”

That's macOS being careful with an app Apple hasn't notarized. Allow Corner once:

  1. “Corner” Not Opened

    Apple could not verify “Corner” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.

    DoneMove to Trash

    1 Click Done

    Not Move to Trash: Corner stays in Applications.

  2. Privacy & Security Security “Corner” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway

    2 Open Anyway

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click Open Anyway next to Corner.

  3. Open “Corner”?

    macOS asks once more before opening an app it couldn't verify.

    Open AnywayDone

    3 Confirm

    Click Open Anyway, then use Touch ID or your password. Corner opens, and macOS won't ask again for this version.

Or skip the prompt: install from Terminal

Paste this into Terminal. It downloads the same disk image, checks its SHA-256 against the one on this site, copies Corner into Applications and opens it. macOS doesn't ask, because the download didn't come through a browser.

curl -fsSL https://corner-mac.vercel.app/install.sh | sh
